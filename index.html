02 Design Process in Fusion 360

  1. Text, Font, Icon - First we choose what our text will be, then our font and an icon of our liking.
  2. Base Sketch - First, we made a reference rectangle that was 300 mm by 120 mm.
  3. Offsets - Outer offsets of about 10 mm and inner offsets of 1.2 mm were added around each character to create the walls.
  4. Extrude - The base plate was extruded to 1 mm, and the outer and inner walls were extruded 30 mm and 20 mm.
  5. LED Passage - Then I created some holes on each letter for the LEDs to pass, but I forgot to make a hole for the LED to come out.
  6. Design Caps - The caps were made with a 1 mm offset and another 0.1 mm inner one, then we extruded 20 mm downward.

The iterations in Fusion 360 are crucial for refining the aesthetics and functionality before moving to the physical printing stage. Below are some illustrative screenshots of the design process, highlighting various stages from initial sketches to finalized models.

03 STL Files & Slicer Settings

The parts were exported as STL files and imported into Bambu Studio for slicing and printing. Supports were enabled for detailed areas, matte PLA filament was used for the base, and the caps were printed differently for better light diffusion.

04 LED Wiring

Bringing the lightbox to life requires careful attention to the LED wiring. The goal is to ensure safe, reliable, and aesthetically pleasing illumination. Most projects utilize addressable WS2812B LED strips, allowing for dynamic lighting effects and color control via a microcontroller.

The wiring process typically involves:

  • Cutting LED Strips: Sizing the strips to fit the internal channels of the 3D printed housing.
  • Soldering Connections: Attaching power (5V), ground (GND), and data (DIN) wires to the LED strips.
  • Microcontroller Integration: Connecting the LED strip's data line to a data pin on a microcontroller (e.g., ESP32 or Arduino Nano) for programming.
  • Power Supply: Ensuring a sufficient 5V power supply to drive the LEDs, calculating current draw based on the number of LEDs used.
  • Diffusion Layer: Placing a translucent 3D printed diffuser between the LEDs and the front plate to ensure a uniform glow.
