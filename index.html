01 Concept & Inspiration

I originally was going to do a 3D Lightbox inspired by I AM MUSIC by Playboi Carti, and the name of his most recent tour ANTAGONIST. I was going to write ANTAGONIST using the font from the album cover from I AM MUSIC and for the icon next to the title I was going to put Carti's famous hand sign YVL. I later changed my design to represent my favorite hockey team, the Calgary Flames. I support this team because my uncle lives in Calgary and he also supports this team. I decided to change the design to represent the team because it would be simpler and faster to do. I also believed the design would look cleaner with the logo of the team being seamlessly integrated into the text.

02 Design Process in Fusion 360

  1. 01 Text, Font, Icon - First we choose what our text will be, then our font and an icon of our liking.
  2. 02 Base Sketch - First, we made a reference rectangle that was 300 mm by 120 mm.
  3. 03 Offsets - Outer offsets of about 10 mm and inner offsets of 1.2 mm were added around each character to create the walls.
  4. 04 Extrude - The base plate was extruded to 1 mm, and the outer and inner walls were extruded 30 mm and 20 mm.
  5. 05 LED Passage - Then I created some holes on each letter for the LEDs to pass, but I forgot to make a hole for the LED to come out.
  6. 06 Design Caps - The caps were made with a 1 mm offset and another 0.1 mm inner one, then we extruded 20 mm downward.

03 STL Files and Slicer Settings

The parts were exported as STL files and imported into Bambu Studio for slicing and printing. Supports were enabled for detailed areas, matte PLA filament was used for the base, and the caps were printed differently for better light diffusion.

06 Reflection

What worked for my design was that the letters fit well even though you had to force them a little bit. I also liked how the base and the first hole from the C to the A ended up. What I would change would be the flames from the C. The flames either ended up too tight, or would not fit at all. The bottom and top flame both fit, but were very tight and you would have to force them. On the other hand, the middle flame did not fit at all, not even if you force it. I would also make the rest of the holes in the base bigger, but I am not sure because I was not able to test if the LEDs fit, but just to make sure I would make them bigger.
